# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can contribute regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Things that make this project better:

- Being clear and direct about code without being unkind about people
- Taking a disagreement about a technical decision as a technical disagreement
- Explaining the reasoning behind feedback, so it teaches rather than just judges
- Assuming a question is asked in good faith, especially from someone new
- Owning mistakes and correcting them plainly

Things that are not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commits — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers via
[GitHub Security Advisories](../../security/advisories/new) or a direct message.
Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and
will explain why. Repeated or severe violations lead to a temporary or
permanent ban from the project.

Maintainers who do not act in good faith when enforcing this document may face
consequences determined by the project's leadership.
